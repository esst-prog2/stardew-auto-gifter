- 2026-10-04: Started a spike (on the `hw4-spike` branch) into the earlier "anchor chest color sometimes disappears" mystery. Question: does a chest marked with the sentinel color `(13, 202, 91)` keep that color after its own menu is closed and reopened, and after a save/reload? Hypothesis: the vanilla `ItemGrabMenu` constructor recalculates the chest's displayed color via `DiscreteColorPicker.getSelectionFromColor` when the chest's menu opens, and because `(13, 202, 91)` doesn't match any of the 21 vanilla swatches, that lookup returns 0 and overwrites the color to `(0, 0, 0)` - meaning simply opening and closing the chest's own menu, not only picking it up, could be enough to un-mark it. Method: read the existing chest-mark diagnostic log's "color before" value from two tests - (1) close and reopen the chest's menu, (2) save and reload - and treat either `(13, 202, 91)` (hypothesis false, color held) or `(0, 0, 0)` (hypothesis true, color was reset) as the result. Decided by the user.
- 2026-10-04: The existing chest-mark diagnostic only logged color on a G-keypress, which couldn't test the spike's hypothesis (color changing just from opening the menu, with no keypress). Added a temporary `Display.MenuChanged` hook in `ModEntry.cs` that logs any chest's position and `playerChoiceColor` every time its own menu opens, tagged `[hw4-spike]`, to be removed once the spike is resolved. Decided by the user, implemented by the assistant.
- 2026-10-04: Spike result: the hypothesis is not supported. On the same chest (`{X:70 Y:11}`), the sentinel color stayed `(13, 202, 91)` after closing and reopening its menu, and again `(13, 202, 91)` after a save and reload - never `(0, 0, 0)`, in both of the two tests. The vanilla `ItemGrabMenu`/`DiscreteColorPicker.getSelectionFromColor` interaction is ruled out as the cause of the earlier "color sometimes disappears" reports. See `spike/sentinel-color-evidence.md` for the quoted log lines and the code-location argument, and `spike/sentinel-test1-reopen.txt` / `spike/sentinel-test2-save-reload.txt` for the raw logs. Decided by the user.
