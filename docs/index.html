Walk past. They get their gift.

Auto-Gifter is a SMAPI mod for Stardew Valley. In the evening you see tomorrow's gift plan; the next day, when you walk past a villager on the plan, the mod gives them their gift from your chests. No button to press.

Plan tonight. Sleep. Just walk tomorrow.

  1. Evening: press F8

    On the NPCs tab, tick the villagers you want to gift. The Plan tab shows tomorrow's gift for each of them.

  2. Change your mind

    Click a row to "[ ]" to leave that villager out tomorrow, for example when you want to give them something yourself.

  3. Next day: just walk

    Come within 1.5 tiles of a villager on the plan. One item leaves your chests, and a message tells you.

Plan tab[x] Haley -> Coconut

Plans always start the next day. Ticking a villager does not change today.

How it picks the gift

1 · Marked chest firstThe loved item you have the most of there, at least two. No loved item? A liked one (you can turn liked items off).
2 · Then the other farm chestsOnly if the marked chest has nothing: the same search through every chest on the farm and in its barn, coop, shed and greenhouse.
3 · Or the marked chest onlySwitch on "Only use the marked chest" in Settings and step 2 is skipped.

It never takes what you want to keep.

  • Blocklist. Tick an item on the Blocklist tab, such as a Prismatic Shard, and it is left out of every plan worked out after that. Tomorrow's plan is worked out when you open F8, so after changing the Blocklist, close F8 and open it again. A gift already planned for today is not affected.

  • One always stays. An item is only given if you have at least two of it, so you never lose your last one.

  • The game's limits, respected. At most one gift per villager per day and two per week, checked again right before the hand-off.

  • An off switch. The mod only works while exactly one chest is marked. Open a chest on your farm and press G to mark it; it is renamed "Auto-Gifter Chest". Press G again to unmark it, and gifting stops.

  • Villagers you have not met are skipped. Their row says "Emily: not met yet - talk to them first".

  • It tells you when it can't. If the mod has nothing it can give a villager today, or they already got two gifts this week, they tell you so when you walk up, once a day.

  • Only farm chests. Chests in the farmhouse and its cellar are never touched.

How it compares

Other gifting mods exist. Some send gifts from a chest when you press a key; others remind you who to gift. Auto-Gifter does not need a key press, and it picks the item for you: the one you have the most of.

Install and first day

  1. Install SMAPI.
  2. Download the zip from the v0.1.0 Release and unzip it into your game's Mods folder.
  3. Start the game and load your save. The first time you step out onto your farm, you get a chest and a short welcome message.
  4. Place the chest outside on your farm, or in a barn, coop, shed or greenhouse (not in the farmhouse). Open it and press G. The chest is renamed "Auto-Gifter Chest": that means it is marked.
  5. Press F8 and tick the villagers you want to gift. Gifting starts the next day.

The keys and the liked-item fallback are in config.json (MenuKey, ChestMarkKey, IncludeLikedItems). The Settings tab of the F8 menu has "Only use the marked chest (ignore other farm chests)".

Known limits

  • Single player only; not tested in multiplayer.
  • F8 does nothing while a chest or another menu is open. Close it first.
  • Changes always apply from the next day. After changing the Blocklist, reopen F8 so tomorrow's plan is worked out again.
  • This is version 0.1.0, an early release.
  • No gameplay recording yet: a walk-through clip is planned for December 2026.
