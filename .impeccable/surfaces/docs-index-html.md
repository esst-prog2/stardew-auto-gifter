## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one night seen through a farmhouse window: you plan tomorrow in the dark, then scroll into the morning when the gifts are handed over. It refuses the category default of a screenshot banner over a feature list.

OWN-WORLD: Deep indigo night (#1c1f4a) that brightens down the page into morning sky (#8fd3ff); window panes as the only containers, lit amber (#ffb627) for planned, dark indigo for skipped, dashed outline for not met yet; white text; a sturdy warm grotesk display face, self-hosted, and a hyperlegible body face. No cards, no boxes outside the window grid, no game art.

STORY: A player sees that gifts are planned tonight and handed over tomorrow with no button press, trusts that rare items stay safe and the limits are stated, and downloads v0.1.0.
